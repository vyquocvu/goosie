## Evidence limits

The two parity rows share one incident; the two stylesheet rows share one incident; dependency and test-flag rows share one incident. No PR numbers were inferred. REL-01 proves initial readiness, not post-action frame freshness. REL-04 lacks a dedicated extent-only oracle. REL-07's current focused test is not a complete wrapped-row oracle. REL-09's existing tests do not separately prove media invalidation. New behavior needs its own measured oracle rather than overstating these tests.

An Important finding needs reproduction or a concrete missing verification contract. Nits do not block. Call a failure pre-existing only after reproduction against the same base/environment, and keep a failing required check visibly unverified.
